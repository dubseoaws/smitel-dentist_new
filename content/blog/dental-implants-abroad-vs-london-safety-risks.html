Planning, Aftercare and What Happens if Problems Arise

Implant treatment usually follows a sequence of stages: initial assessment and imaging, any preparatory work (such as extractions or bone grafting), implant placement, a healing period to allow the implant to integrate with the bone, and finally the fitting of the permanent restoration. Review appointments are typically scheduled at intervals during and after treatment to monitor progress.

When treatment is carried out abroad, attending follow-up appointments can be more difficult. Returning to the overseas clinic for reviews or to address any concerns involves additional travel, time and cost. If a complication arises after returning to the UK â€” such as infection, implant loosening or a problem with the restoration â€” managing the situation can be more complex.

It is always worth requesting a full written record of your treatment, including the implant brand, dimensions and batch numbers, from any overseas provider.

Questions to Ask Any Implant Clinic (UK or Abroad)

Regardless of where you are considering having treatment, the following questions can help you evaluate any implant provider:

  • What are the qualifications and experience of the clinician who will place the implants?
  • Is the clinician registered with a regulatory body, and can you verify this?
  • Which implant brand and system will be used, and is long-term clinical data available for it?
  • Will you receive a written treatment plan that explains each stage, the timeline and the costs?
  • What consent process is followed, and will you have time to consider the plan before committing?
